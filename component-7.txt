
### 5. Copy the source files

Copy the files from this project into your `src/` directory, keeping the same
paths:

- `src/App.tsx`
- `src/data/assets.ts`
- `src/components/Header.tsx`
- `src/components/StatStrip.tsx`
- `src/components/AssetToolbar.tsx`
- `src/components/AssetList.tsx`
- `src/components/AssetDetail.tsx`

Make sure `src/lib/utils.ts` exists (shadcn creates it during `init`) — it
exports the `cn` helper used across the components.
